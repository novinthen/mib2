# MIB 2.0 Governance Continuity Standard

**Control status:** Internal governance design pending Cabinet and agency confirmation. It does not establish a body, delegate a statutory power, create a post or authorise expenditure.

The Prime Minister is the programme sponsor and strategic reviewer, not its daily operating system. Each control below is generated from `GOVERNANCE_CONTINUITY_REGISTER.csv`.

## GC-01 — Prime Ministerial sponsorship and strategic review

**Status:** `draft_pending_cabinet_confirmation`

| Control field | Controlled position |
|---|---|
| Continuity rule | The Prime Minister sponsors the policy direction and reviews strategic performance, but routine coordination, reporting and corrective action continue under the delegated chain whether or not the Prime Minister personally chairs a scheduled review |
| Primary owner | Prime Minister |

| Activation trigger | Cabinet adopts the governance model; applies continuously and whenever a Prime Ministerial review is postponed |

## GC-06 — Automatic milestone escalation

**Status:** `draft_pending_cabinet_confirmation`

| Control field | Controlled position |
|---|---|
| Continuity rule | A missed or forecast-missed milestone automatically enters the exception register and advances through the ministry delivery officer, accounting officer, senior-officials committee, designated minister and Cabinet according to pre-approved thresholds without waiting for a quarterly political meeting |
| Primary owner | Each responsible accounting officer |
| Delegated operating owner | Secretariat exception-control lead |

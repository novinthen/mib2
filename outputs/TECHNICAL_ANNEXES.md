## Annex B — Worked derivations

**B.1 Preschool eligible cohort (ASM-008).** Indian population ≈ 6.5% × 34.4m ≈ 2.24m. Applying the national crude birth rate gives an annual Indian birth cohort of ≈20,000, so ages 4–5 ≈ 40,000 children. Applying the MIB-era bottom-40% household share (39%) gives ≈15,600 → planning range 15,000–17,000, midpoint **16,000**.
*Phase 1 cost check:* 16,000 × 0.40 × RM4,200 × 2 years = **RM53.76m** ≈ 53.800 as modelled.
*Phase 2:* 16,000 × 0.70 × 4,200 × 2 = **RM94.08m** ≈ 94.100. *Phase 3:* 16,000 × 0.80 × 4,200 × 2 = **RM107.52m** ≈ 107.500.
*Limitation:* applies a 2014-vintage income share to a 2026 population. **Provisional** (VAL-09).

**B.2 Civil service target (ASM-020).** Base 1,302,429 (31 Dec 2024, excluding RMP and MAF).
At 3.7%: 48,190 officers. At 7.0%: 91,170. Difference **42,980 net additions**, ≈7,160/year for six years → **rejected**.
At 4.2%: 54,702 → 6,512 net additions. At 4.8%: 62,517 → 14,327. **Adopted range 4.2%–4.8%.**
Reported as *both* a share and a headcount because the denominator will change independently of Indian recruitment (ASM-021).

**B.3 Indian household denominator (CLM-062).** 227,600 ÷ 0.39 = 583,590. Cross-check against MIB p.17: 57,624 = 10%, 71,148 = 12%, 112,896 = 19% → implied totals 576,240 / 592,900 / 594,189. **Consistent.** 2014 basis; requires DOSM re-estimation (VAL-02).

**B.4 TVET pipeline (ASM-C06).** Per enrolee: stipend RM500 × 12 = RM6,000 + programme support RM2,500 = **RM8,500**.
Phase 1: 5,000 × 8,500 = RM42.5m + (1,000 × RM4,000) = **RM46.5m**.
Phase 2: 10,000 × 8,500 = RM85m + (2,000 × 4,000) = **RM93.0m**.
Phase 3: 15,000 × 8,500 = RM127.5m + (3,000 × 4,000) = **RM139.5m**. Cumulative 30,000 enrolees.

**B.5 Navigator cadre (ASM-C09).** Caseload 150 households/navigator; loaded cost RM72,000/yr.
Phase 1: 120 × 72,000 × 2 = RM17.28m + RM12m system = **RM29.28m** ≈ 29.300.
Phase 2: 280 × 72,000 × 2 = RM40.32m + RM4m = **RM44.32m** ≈ 44.300.
Phase 3: 400 × 72,000 × 2 = RM57.6m + RM2m = **RM59.6m**. Concurrent service capacity ≈60,000 households; cumulative reach depends on case duration and turnover and is not specified.

**B.6 SJKT (ASM-C03).** Audit 528 × RM25,000 = RM13.2m. Digital facilities 528 × RM85,000 = RM44.9m. Remediation: 30% of 528 = 158.4 schools (30% of 528) × RM450,000 = RM71.280m, phased 30/40/30. Coordinator training 528 × 2 × RM3,500 = RM3.7m.
*The 55% existing-funding assumption is provisional and gating (VAL-11).*

**B.7 Secretariat (ASM-C15).** 35 posts × RM110,000 loaded = RM3.85m/yr + RM2m operating = RM5.85m/yr = **RM11.7m per two-year phase**.

**B.8 Beneficiary overlap (ASM-030).** Household-level programmes (PRG-09 ≈60,000; PRG-11 ≈50,000; PRG-12 ≈6,000) sum to 116,000 *reach*. Applying the assumed overlap factor 0.45 gives an estimated **≈64,000 unique households**. **Reach is never summed across programmes in the proposal or the dashboard.** The factor is provisional and is replaced by observed data once the household record operates (VAL-25).

## Annex C — Responsibility matrix
Full detail in `RESPONSIBILITY_MATRIX.csv` (16 rows, one per retained substantive programme). Mandate verification status: 10 consistent-requires-confirmation, 4 requires-establishment (PRG-09, PRG-11, PRG-15 and PRG-16), and **2 contested** (PRG-04 and PRG-12 — state jurisdiction over land, local government and religious institutions).

Three constitutional and institutional limits constrain what may be promised:
1. **Citizenship** determinations under Article 15A / Article 19 / Second Schedule Part II rest with the Minister. No clearance percentage may be committed (PRG-01).
2. **Appointment** to the public service rests with the Public Service Commission, constitutionally independent of the executive. PRG-13 addresses application supply and preparation only, with no adjustment to entry standards.
3. **Land** is a state matter. PRG-04 and PRG-12 proceed only by state consent, and tenure findings are referred, not determined.
